Objective Establish Meridian as the undisputed daily habit for frontline field crews, turning raw field activity into high-value compliance data.

0 - 3 Months

Outcome Bet: We bet creating a separate, lightweight mobile-only app container for foremen will eliminate corporate back-office administrative clutter and drive high-frequency daily adoption for active field crews.

Success Signal • Weekly Active Users (WAU) on mobile increases by 35%

Offline-First Mobile Experience

Outcome Bet: We bet building a robust offline-first synchronization engine with local SQLite caching and background syncing will prevent daily log upload failures (dropping failure rate from 12% to < 1%) for foremen working on job sites with poor connectivity.

Success Signal • Log upload failure rate drops from 12% to < 1%
• 100% offline logs successfully sync upon reconnect

Outcome Bet: We bet reducing the required daily log form fields from 14 to 4 critical compliance items will slash the average log submission time from 25 minutes to under 3 minutes (maintaining 95%+ data completeness) for busy foremen at the end of their shifts.

Success Signal • Average logging time drops from 25 to < 3 mins
• Data completeness rating remains at 95%+
